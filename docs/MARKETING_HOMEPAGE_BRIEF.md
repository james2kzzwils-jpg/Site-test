# Marketing homepage brief

Updated: 2026-07-17
Base branch: `redesignLinda`
Related roadmap: `docs/MARKETING_SITE_ROADMAP.md`

This brief is the **designer-facing handoff** for the marketing homepage.
It should be usable without needing client-portal context.

---

## 1. Project intent

Design a public homepage for **Epov Creative Labs** that feels:
- premium
- controlled
- cinematic
- technically precise
- founder-led
- not generic

The homepage must balance two truths at once:
1. this is a **founder-led creative lab** with a real person behind the work
2. this is **not** a casual freelancer portfolio

The page should feel like a high-end creative practice with discipline, authorship, and production depth.

---

## 2. Brand model to design around

### Working public model
**Epov Creative Labs — founder-led creative lab by Andrey Epov**

### What the design should communicate
- strong authorship
- premium CGI / motion capability
- technical seriousness
- calm confidence
- visual restraint
- clear commercial usefulness

### What the design should avoid
- fake big-agency energy
- generic Behance-style freelancer presentation
- startup SaaS UI language
- overdesigned motion that makes the message harder to read
- influencer/lifestyle photography tone

---

## 3. Core emotional axis

### Brand line
**Giving Form to the Invisible. Precision in service of Intention.**

This line is the emotional / conceptual axis.
It should inform mood, composition, atmosphere, and tone.

### Important
The homepage still needs commercial clarity.
This line should not replace a clear explanation of what the studio does.

---

## 4. Hero direction — approved starting point

### Direction
**Balanced**

### Hero logic
- the hero should feel cinematic and authored
- but the message should still be clear within seconds
- projects should be discovered by scroll, not by a dedicated hero CTA

### Hero CTA decision
- **Primary CTA:** Showreel
- **Secondary CTA:** Start a Project
- **No hero button:** View Projects / View Selected Work

### Scroll logic
The user should naturally continue from the hero into **Selected Works**.
The layout should make the downward path feel obvious and elegant.

---

## 5. Homepage structure

Design the homepage in this order:

1. **Hero**
2. **Proof / Trust Strip**
3. **Selected Works**
4. **Services**
5. **Why Clients Choose This Setup**
6. **Process**
7. **About the Founder**
8. **FAQ**
9. **Final CTA**

Each block should feel like part of one cinematic system, but each must also have a clear job in the conversion funnel.

---

## 6. Section-by-section design brief

### 6.1 Hero

#### Purpose
Explain who this is, what is being offered, and why it feels premium.

#### Must communicate
- founder-led identity
- premium CGI / product visuals / procedural motion
- cinematic confidence
- clear first action: showreel

#### Visual direction
- dark, low-key, controlled environment
- strong type hierarchy
- subtle depth / atmosphere behind content
- the founder may be integrated into the visual world
- no clutter
- no “portfolio template” feeling

#### Founder presence options
Preferred order to explore:
1. side profile / silhouette presence
2. integrated background presence with respectful distance
3. controlled portrait fragment, not a loud front-facing headshot

#### Avoid
- smiling startup portrait
- loud lifestyle image
- making the founder image compete with the headline
- too many interactive choices in the first screen

---

### 6.2 Proof / Trust Strip

#### Purpose
Quickly reduce doubt after the hero.

#### Role in composition
A narrow, elegant strip that reinforces positioning, trust, and technical specificity.

#### Content examples
- Founder-Led Creative Lab
- Premium CGI
- Product Visuals
- Procedural Motion
- Houdini-Driven Workflows
- Remote Worldwide

#### Design note
This should feel more like a premium signal band than a noisy announcement ticker.
If motion is used, it should remain calm and deliberate.

---

### 6.3 Selected Works

#### Purpose
Prove range and quality through a focused editorial selection.

#### Strategy
Do not present this like an undifferentiated project archive.
Use a stronger editorial hierarchy built around 3 flagship directions:
- **Scale**
- **Luxury**
- **Innovation**

#### What the block should achieve
- show visual excellence
- show project diversity without chaos
- make each flagship feel like a proof category, not just a tile

#### Design note
This block should be rich, but still readable.
Scrolling into it should feel like entering the core proof layer of the site.

---

### 6.4 Services

#### Purpose
Clarify what can be hired.

#### Key service lines
- CGI Product Animation
- Houdini Simulations
- Procedural Motion Systems

#### Design direction
- do not over-prioritize tools visually
- outcome and application should lead
- technology can support the block as proof

#### Visual note
The section may feel sculptural / cinematic, but clarity must win over spectacle.

---

### 6.5 Why Clients Choose This Setup

#### Purpose
Translate the founder-led model into decision-making confidence.

#### Themes to visualize
- technical predictability
- premium look development
- transparent checkpoints
- procedural control
- remote production readiness
- founder-level attention to detail

#### Design note
This block can feel structured, almost architectural.
It should communicate reliability, not noise.

---

### 6.6 Process

#### Purpose
Make complex production feel understandable and controlled.

#### Working process structure
- Discovery
- References / Moodboard
- Animatic / Direction Lock
- Lookdev / Simulation
- Final Render / Delivery

#### Design direction
- clear rhythm
- reduced abstraction
- visible progression
- elegant system, not corporate flowchart

---

### 6.7 About the Founder

#### Purpose
Introduce the person behind the work and strengthen trust.

#### Must communicate
- this is a real, high-level practice led by a person with taste and technical depth
- the founder is calm, deliberate, and serious about craft

#### Founder visual priority
This is the strongest place for a direct portrait.

#### Tone
- not self-promotional in a loud way
- not humble to the point of disappearing
- not casual
- not over-mystified

#### Suggested emotional outcome
“Now I understand who I would be working with.”

---

### 6.8 FAQ

#### Purpose
Handle practical objections without dropping the tone.

#### Priority themes
- timing
- how estimates work
- revisions
- deliverables / source files
- remote / international work
- approvals

#### Design note
The FAQ should feel elegant and compressed, not bulky or default.

---

### 6.9 Final CTA

#### Purpose
Convert interest into contact.

#### Must communicate
- the studio is approachable
- the next step is clear
- the founder is present
- the interaction feels premium, not desperate

#### Channels
- Telegram
- Email
- Calendly

#### Visual direction
This block can carry a more direct founder presence than the hero.
It should feel like a calm invitation into a working relationship.

---

## 7. Founder photography brief

### Required mood
- low-key
- composed
- high-control
- premium restraint
- tactile but clean

### Required environment cues
- dark interior
- textured wall / wood / stone / concrete
- no visual clutter
- no bright lifestyle setup

### Required wardrobe cues
- monochrome
- simple, high-quality garments
- no loud graphics
- no trend-chasing styling

### Shot priorities
1. **Hero** — side profile / stillness / breathing loop possibility
2. **About** — direct portrait with eye contact
3. **CTA** — respectful direct presence
4. **Optional support** — seated at table / hands / craft posture

### Hard no
- influencer pose language
- coworking/startup clichés
- smiling sales portrait
- hyper-fashion editorial that overwhelms the service message

---

## 8. Art-direction references

### Lean into
- luxury watchmaking precision
- architectural minimalism
- low-key cinematic lighting
- disciplined materials
- controlled empty space
- exact, quiet confidence

### Emotional reference words
- precision
- intention
- composure
- authorship
- atmosphere
- engineered beauty

### Avoid
- noisy futurism
- generic dark-mode tech aesthetics
- too much chrome / glow without purpose
- maximalist motion clutter

---

## 9. Mobile design principles

### Must remain true on mobile
- message clarity in first screen
- visible showreel CTA
- visible start-project CTA
- obvious scroll path into selected work
- clean founder presence where used

### Mobile constraints
- no dependence on hover-only information
- no heavy interactions required to understand the offer
- no horizontal-scroll dependency for essential content
- complex motion should gracefully degrade

---

## 10. Success criteria for the designer

The homepage direction is working when:
- a visitor understands the offer in about 5 seconds
- the page feels founder-led, not anonymous
- the page feels premium, not generic
- the page does not pretend to be a huge team
- the visual system supports trust as much as aesthetics
- scroll from hero into selected work feels natural
- showreel feels like the correct first action
- the design can later support richer motion without needing a full redesign
